# Device Monitor v2.15.1 — Service retirement correction

Device Monitor v2.15.1 preserves responding infrastructure services without treating an
IP-only response as proof of a device identity, and makes service retirement safe against
concurrent refreshes and partial alert cleanup.

## Changes

- Service verification records endpoint presence on the service row. It advances
  `devices.last_seen` only when the caller supplies a MAC/IP association observed with that
  probe.
- A recent successful non-authoritative probe protects its service row from retirement even
  when Hostwatch has no matching device claim. Failed observations, authoritative/configuration
  rows and missing or unparseable timestamps do not qualify.
- Service freshness uses the same inclusive, whole-second retirement cutoff as device
  inventory, including when the injected clock has microseconds. Legacy unqualified timestamps
  retain the existing conservative UTC/local reader.
- Retirement selection, service deletion and pending-alert cleanup run under one
  `BEGIN IMMEDIATE` transaction after the separately committed status-ageing update. A
  pending-alert deletion failure rolls the service deletion back.
- The local deployment wrapper passes the validated `.23` hostname to the installer rather than
  the dispatch alias.

## Upgrade Path

The installer accepts `2.8` through `2.15.0` as direct predecessors. Version 2.15.0 was added
as the immediate predecessor; all earlier established paths are retained.
